Why can't I add another 30-minute task?

The example block has 120 minutes. Existing tasks use 96 minutes, leaving only 24. A 30-minute task would require 126 minutes total, which is 6 minutes over capacity. This page demonstrates the rule; validation will be built into the functional VibeFlow version.
