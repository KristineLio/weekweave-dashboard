WHAT WE ACTUALLY NEED

Four steps. One honest plan.

Most to-do lists keep counting. Weekweave helps you decide what can really fit—and what can wait.

01 / RESPECT REAL LIFE
◷

Block the hours you don't have.

Add work, classes, or appointments as Busy. A 09:00–20:00 shift is eleven hours reserved, not eleven hours for new tasks.

02 / PLAN WITH LIMITS
▦

Give work the time it deserves.

Create a Focus block, assign tasks and see what remains. Tasks that exceed a block's minutes or overlap a schedule are rejected.

03 / EXECUTE & ADJUST
✓

Finish something. Free the space.

Move work through Inbox → Planned → Scheduled → Done. Completed tasks stop reserving minutes but remain in your history.

04 / PROVE YOUR PROGRESS
✳

Make the week teach you.

Track habits, log evidence toward measurable goals, and save one useful reflection to guide the next week.
